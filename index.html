Louis Owner Mail

Louis Owner Mail is a private, single-owner Gmail integration for Louis Winthorpe III, a locally operated personal AI assistant.

What it does

  • Reads only messages matching the configured owner-to-Louis addresses.
  • Places authenticated messages into a private local review queue.
  • Creates owner-only drafts whose complete contents are bound to an exact approval fingerprint.
  • Sends a draft only after the owner approves that exact fingerprint.

The integration has no public users, advertising, data brokerage, autonomous sending, attachment access, deletion authority, or mailbox-management authority.
